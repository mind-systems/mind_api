Align the two controllers: change the signature from `trackActivity(request: Observable<StateRequest>, metadata?: Metadata)` to `trackActivity(request: Observable<StateRequest>, @GrpcCurrentUser() user: JwtPayload | null)`; remove the internal `(metadata as any)[GRPC_USER_KEY]` extraction; update the null check to use `user` directly. No behavior change — purely a test-ergonomics and consistency improvement.
